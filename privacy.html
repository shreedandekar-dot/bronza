Bronza Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Summary

Bronza keeps your personal information on your phone. We have no user accounts and no servers that store your profile, photos, health answers or session history.

  • Your skin profile, sensitivity answers, session history, progress photos and settings stay on your device.
  • Selfie scans are analysed on your device and deleted straight away. They are never uploaded.
  • To show UV forecasts, your phone sends its location to our weather data provider. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We receive confirmation of your subscription, never your card details.
  • We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we are. Bronza is operated by Shree Ajit Dandekar, an individual based in Texas, United States ("we", "us"). Contact: bronza.app.help@gmail.com.

What we collect and where it goes

Most information never leaves your phone; the table shows the few things that do and why.

Goals, timeline, goal shade, quiz answers, skin type Your device only No one To personalise your plan
Medication and skincare sensitivity answers Your device only No one To shorten sessions for safety
Selfie scan photo Analysed on your device, then deleted within seconds No one To suggest your skin tone
Progress photos Your device only, in Bronza's private storage No one, unless you share them To track your tan

Product link clicks Not stored by us Amazon or the retailer you visit, under its own policy Affiliate commissions

Notifications are scheduled on your device. We don't run a notification server.

Images you share (UV cards, stickers) go wherever you send them through your phone's share sheet, under that app's policy.

Analytics and crash reports: We don't currently use analytics or crash reporting. If we add them later, we'll update this policy and the app store privacy labels first.

Sensitive information

Some answers you give Bronza, such as medications, skin reactions and photos of your face, can be health or biometric information. Bronza stores them only on your device, uses them only to calculate your guidance, and never sends them to us or anyone else. The selfie scan reads skin colour only; it does not identify you or create a face template.

Children

Bronza is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. Users aged 13 to 17 get sun safety mode only. Children's first names entered in family mode by a parent stay on the parent's device.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. The only services that receive information are those in the table above, for the purposes listed. We would disclose information only if required by law, and we hold very little to disclose.

Keeping and deleting your information

Information on your device stays until you delete it. Tap Plan > Reset app data to erase everything, including progress photos, or delete the app. Selfie scan photos are deleted automatically within seconds. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their policies; contact us to ask RevenueCat to delete your anonymous purchase record.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security, such as its passcode and encryption. Bronza communicates with its weather and subscription providers over encrypted connections. Keep your phone locked and up to date.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or complain about how we handle it, by emailing bronza.app.help@gmail.com. Because almost everything is on your device, you can also view, change or delete it yourself in the app. We'll reply within 30 days.

  • Australia: we aim to handle personal information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
  • New Zealand: you have rights under the Privacy Act 2020 and can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).
  • United States: depending on your state, you may have rights to access, delete or correct personal information and to opt out of its sale. We don't sell personal information.
  • Europe and the UK: Bronza is not currently offered in the European Union or the United Kingdom. We will add the information required there before offering it.

International transfers. The weather and subscription providers we use may process information in other countries, including the United States and the European Union.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when our practices change, and tell you in the app before any significant change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
