Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Bronza, go to Plan, then Settings, and tap Restore purchase next to Bronza Pro. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
In Bronza, go to Plan, then Settings, and tap Manage next to Bronza Pro. Or open your phone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are made through Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: reportaproblem.apple.com for iPhone, or the Google Play order history for Android. This doesn't affect your rights under consumer law.

How do I delete my data?
Your profile, sessions and photos are stored only on your phone. Go to Plan and tap Reset app data, or delete the app. See our Privacy Policy.

Is Bronza medical advice?
No. Bronza gives estimates based on the UV forecast and your answers. Always get out of the sun if your skin feels hot or turns pink, whatever the timer says. See our Terms of Use.
