Please:

  • Stop and get out of the sun if your skin feels hot, stings, or turns pink, whatever the timer says.

  • Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before tanning if you take medication or use skincare that can increase sun sensitivity, are pregnant, or have a history of skin cancer or a skin condition.
  • See a doctor about any changing mole or spot, severe sunburn, blistering, fever or feeling unwell.
